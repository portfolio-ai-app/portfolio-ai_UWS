How to read the trade plan

The planner converts your risk settings into a simple trading map. Nothing here guarantees a profitable trade.

1. Direction LONG means the model is leaning upward. SHORT means it is leaning downward. WAIT means the signal is not strong enough.
2. Entry The current/reference price used to build the scenario. Your broker's actual execution price may differ.
3. Stop loss Your planned exit if the market moves against the trade. It defines how much price movement you are willing to risk.
4. Take profit Your planned profit target. The selected reward:risk ratio determines how far the target is from the entry relative to the stop.
5. Position size The quantity calculated so that reaching the stop is approximately equal to your chosen money-at-risk budget.
6. Open risk The maximum planned loss across all active setups if every stop were reached, before slippage, gaps, fees or broker-specific effects.
